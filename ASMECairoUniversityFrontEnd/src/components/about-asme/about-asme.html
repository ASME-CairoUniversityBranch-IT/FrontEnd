THE ORGANIZATION

ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) is a globally recognized professional society serving over 100,000 members in 140+ countries. Founded in 1880, ASME facilitates collaboration, knowledge sharing, career enrichment, and skills development across all engineering disciplines.


The Cairo University student branch, one of Egypt's most active chapters, bridges academic learning with real-world engineering practice — equipping members with technical expertise, leadership skills, and professional networks.
